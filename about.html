Our Story

From Two Architects
to a 110-Person Studio

"We started ARKFORM because we were tired of watching beautiful designs die in construction. We wanted to control the entire journey."

In 2008, architect duo Karim Al-Rashidi and Sophia Laurent founded ARKFORM in a shared Dubai studio with one belief: that great commercial design requires both creative vision and construction discipline under the same roof.

Today, our 110-strong team of designers, architects, project managers, and skilled craftspeople delivers landmark projects across the UAE and GCC — maintaining the same exacting standards that have defined us since day one.

Our Values

How We Think
About Design

Value 01
Purposeful Beauty

We believe aesthetics must earn their place by solving a real problem — whether that's wayfinding, brand communication, or creating the right emotional atmosphere for the people who inhabit the space.

Value 02
Radical Transparency

We tell clients what they need to hear, not what they want to hear. Transparent timelines, honest costings, and candid design feedback are non-negotiables at every project stage.

Value 03
Collective Craft

Great spaces are never the result of one person's brilliance — they're the product of a team working together at every scale, from master planning to the choice of a door handle. Every voice matters.

Value 04
Built to Last

We specify materials, structures, and systems for decades, not seasons. A premium interior should look as exceptional in fifteen years as it does on opening day. We design accordingly.

Our Journey

17 Years of Milestones

2008
Studio Founded — Dubai

Karim Al-Rashidi and Sophia Laurent open ARKFORM with a team of four. First project: a 600 sqm law firm fit-out in DIFC.

2011
First 1,000 sqm+ Project

Completed the Nexus Group headquarters — a landmark project that established ARKFORM's reputation for complex office delivery.

2015
Retail Practice Launched

Expanded into retail design with the commission of three flagship stores for a major luxury brand across the GCC.

2018
100th Project Milestone

Celebrated 100 completed projects with a combined area exceeding 250,000 sqm of designed and built commercial space.

2020
LEED Certification Achieved

Became one of the first fit-out firms in the UAE to achieve formal LEED AP certification across the design team.

2022
MENA Design Award — Best Commercial

Awarded the MENA Design Award for Commercial Excellence for the Lumière Flagship Store in Abu Dhabi.

2024
Studio Expansion — 110 Staff

Moved into a new 1,400 sqm studio in Business Bay and grew to a team of 110 designers, architects, and project specialists.
