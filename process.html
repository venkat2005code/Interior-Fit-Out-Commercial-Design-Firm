Why It Matters

Process Is Your
Biggest Insurance Policy

Most commercial fit-out failures are not failures of design — they are failures of process. Budget overruns, missed deadlines, and spaces that don't perform as intended all trace back to a breakdown somewhere in the project management chain.

ARKFORM's 6-step methodology was built over 17 years to eliminate these failure points — through rigorous planning, transparent communication, and single-point accountability at every stage.

The Method

Our Six-Stage Process

01
Discovery & Brief

We immerse ourselves in your business, brand, and operational reality. Site surveys, stakeholder workshops, and detailed benchmarking produce a brief that's uniquely yours — and becomes the foundation for every decision that follows.

02
Concept Design

Our design team translates the brief into a compelling spatial concept. Mood boards, 3D visualisations, material palettes, and narrative flow diagrams are presented and refined through collaborative review sessions until alignment is achieved.

03
Technical Development

The approved concept is developed into fully coordinated technical drawings. Our architects, MEP engineers, and specialist consultants collaborate to ensure every detail is constructable, compliant, and cost-effective before tender.

04
Procurement & Tendering

We manage procurement of every material, fitting, fixture, and furniture item — leveraging our supplier network to secure the right quality at the right price, on timelines that protect your programme.

05
Construction & Fit-Out

Our in-house build team executes with precision. We self-deliver all primary trades and rigorously manage specialist subcontractors. Weekly progress reports, photographic documentation, and client walkthroughs throughout.

06
Handover & Aftercare

We don't deliver keys — we deliver fully commissioned, snagged, and documented spaces. A comprehensive handover pack, system commissioning, and a 5-year aftercare warranty mean we're still your partner long after opening day.

Quality Assurance

Rigorous Quality Inspection

Before we hand over any space, it undergoes our comprehensive 120-point quality inspection. Our independent QA team audits every trade — from MEP systems and lighting controls to joinery tolerances and acoustic seals.

We operate a zero-snag handover policy. This means we don't present the space to you until we are completely satisfied that it meets our exacting standards.

The Final Step

Seamless Project Handover

Handover isn't just handing over keys. It involves full systems training for your facilities management team, O&M manuals, warranty certificates, and as-built drawings.

Our Day 2 support team remains on-call during your first month of occupancy to ensure a smooth transition into your new commercial environment.

FAQ

Questions Before
You Commit

How long does a commercial fit-out typically take?+

A standard office fit-out of 1,000–2,000 sqm takes 10–16 weeks from design sign-off to handover. Larger or more complex projects are typically 16–24 weeks. We provide a detailed programme of works at the outset of every project so you know exactly what to expect.

Can you work around our existing business operations?+

Yes. We specialise in phased delivery programmes that allow your business to continue operating during construction. Our team works flexible hours — including evenings and weekends — to minimise disruption wherever possible.

What is your standard payment structure?+

Payment milestones are agreed at project outset and tied to programme deliverables — concept sign-off, technical drawings, construction completion, and handover. There are no surprise costs; our BOQ is transparent and detailed.

Do you handle projects outside the UAE?+

Yes. We have delivered projects across the GCC and internationally, including Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UK, and Singapore. Our project management systems are designed for remote oversight with the same rigour as local delivery.

What happens if the project runs over budget or schedule?+

Our detailed pre-contract BOQ and programme are designed to prevent this. In the rare event of a variation, we provide written change orders for your approval before any additional cost is incurred. No surprises — ever.
